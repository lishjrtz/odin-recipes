Hearty Six-Can Chili

This recipe is one that has remained in my family for generations. Its quick, absurdly simple, and a fantastic base for developing your own signature chili!

Ingredients

  • 1 pound lean ground beef (90%)
  • 1/2 cup onion, chopped
  • 2 15.5oz cans chili beans in sauce, undrained
  • 2 14.5oz cans diced tomatoes, undrained
  • 1 15oz can black beans, drained and rinsed
  • 1 10.75oz can condensed cream of mushroom soup
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder

Steps

  1. Place ground beef and onion in Dutch Oven. Cook and stir over medium heat 8-10 minutes, or until beef is no longer pink, breaking beef into small crumbles. Drain, if necessary.
  2. Add chili beans, tomatoes, black beans, mushroom soup, and chili powder to Dutch Oven.
  3. Bring to a boil, reduce heat, and simmer covered at low heat 30 minutes, stirring occasionally.
